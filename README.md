## 🌟 About the Band

> **Minerva** is a prominent Bangladeshi groove and thrash metal band formed in Dhaka in 2009. After winning the *Incursion Battle of the Bands 2011*, Minerva established themselves as an influential powerhouse in modern Bangladeshi heavy metal with crushing guitar riffs, dynamic groove structures, and socially conscious lyricism.

### 📖 About the Album
Released in July 2013 under Incursion Music, *Biday Songbidhan* (*"Farewell to the Constitution"*) is Minerva's critically acclaimed debut studio album. Produced, mixed, and mastered by guitarist Sultan Rafsan Khan, the album confronts social corruption, rigid societal barriers, and political turmoil across eight tracks in Bengali and English.
